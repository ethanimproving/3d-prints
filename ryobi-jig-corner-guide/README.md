# Vocabulary

<table>
  <tr><th>What You&#39;d Call It</th><th>Engineering Term</th><th>Purpose</th></tr>
  <tr><td>Front block</td><td>Contact pad / Locator pad</td><td>Touches the workpiece and establishes position</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Curved section</td><td>Curved support arm</td><td>Connects the locator pad to the mounting feature while saving material</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Curve radius</td><td>Fillet radius / Arc radius</td><td>Provides strength and smooth load transfer</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Bottom triangle</td><td>Reinforcing rib / Gusset</td><td>Prevents flexing or snapping</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Two &quot;lips&quot; that slide into the jig</td><td>Mounting rails / Guide rails</td><td>Engage the mating slot in the jig</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Gap between the rails</td><td>Slot / Clearance slot</td><td>Allows the part to slide onto a mating tongue or track</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Vertical walls around the slot</td><td>Rail walls</td><td>Provide alignment and retention</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Small overhang under the curve</td><td>Retention lip / Catch feature</td><td>Helps prevent pullout</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Flat side surfaces</td><td>Datum surfaces</td><td>Reference surfaces used for locating the part</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Rounded corners</td><td>Edge fillets</td><td>Reduce stress concentration and improve molding</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Total thickness</td><td>Overall depth</td><td>Front-to-back dimension</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Overall width</td><td>Overall span</td><td>Left-to-right dimension</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Overall height</td><td>Overall height</td><td>Top-to-bottom dimension</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Internal hollow areas</td><td>Relief pockets</td><td>Save plastic and reduce sink marks</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Thin walls inside</td><td>Stiffening ribs</td><td>Increase strength without adding much material</td></tr>
</table>
